Great Personalities who Visited Contai

Over the centuries, men of stature and distinction have set foot in different parts of the Sub-division, making the area hallowed.

Guru Nanak (16th Century)

While traveling to Ganga-Sagar, Guru Nanak visited the Contai area. He took shelter at "Baro-bazar" (near the present Sub-divisional Hospital). A Gurudwara was later built to commemorate his visit.

Sri Chaitanya (1539)

On his way to Puri, Sri Chaitanya crossed the Hooghly river and passed through Tamluk and the Keleyghai valley. It is said he rested under a Nim tree at Patharghata in Patashpur.

Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay (1860)

The literary giant worked as the Deputy Magistrate of Contai in 1860. While investigating a robbery at Dariapur near the Rasulpur estuary, the scenic beauty inspired him to write his famous novel Kapalkundala.

Mahatma Gandhi (1925 & 1945)

1st Visit (1925): Gandhi stayed at the Contai National School. He held a prayer meeting at Darua Maidan, advising people to fight untouchability and use Khadi.
2nd Visit (1945): After the devasting cyclone of 1942 and the famine of 1943, Gandhi visited to witness the people's suffering. He stayed for three days, visiting Contai and the weaver's village at Krishnanagar.

Subhas Chandra Bose (1938)

Netaji visited Contai on April 12, 1938. He was given a 12-gun salute at the northern gate of the town (Sasmal Gate). He addressed meetings at Khejuri and Balighai, urging the youth to lead the freedom struggle.
