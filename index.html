History of Contai (Kanthi)

In the 15th century, during the visit of Fa-Hien, Contai was uninhabited. Later known as Kendua, a harbour, it eventually evolved into Contai (Kanthi). Some suggest the name originates from the "Kanth" (long sand-walls) protecting the area, while others trace it to wandering saints asking "Kanha thee?" (Where are you from?).

Today, it is a bustling sub-divisional town with a rich heritage of freedom fighters and culture.
